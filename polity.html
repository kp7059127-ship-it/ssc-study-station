🇮🇳 Indian Polity - Top 50 PYQs for SSC

Selected from last 5 year SSC papers

Q1. Constitution Assembly first meeting?
Ans: 9 Dec 1946 - Sachchidanand Sinha (Temporary President)
Q2. Constitution banne me kitna time laga?
Ans: 2 Year 11 Month 18 Days
Q3. Preamble me kitni baar sanshodhan?
Ans: 1 baar, 42nd Amendment 1976 me
Q4. Fundamental Rights kahan hai?
Ans: Part III, Article 12-35 (USA se liye)
Q5. DPSP kahan hai?
Ans: Part IV, Article 36-51 (Ireland se)
Q6. Fundamental Duties?
Ans: 42nd Amendment se jode, ab 11 Duties (Article 51A)
Q7. President ka Article?
Ans: Article 52 - Executive head
Q8. Vice President?
Ans: Article 63
Q9. Speaker ko kaun hatata hai?
Ans: Lok Sabha apni majority se - Art 94
Q10. Rajya Sabha kaisi hai?
Ans: Permanent House, kabhi bhang nahi hoti
Q11. Supreme Court kab bana?
Ans: 28 Jan 1950, Art 124
Q12. CAG ka Article?
Ans: Article 148
Q13. Finance Commission?
Ans: Article 280
Q14. Panchayati Raj?
Ans: 73rd Amendment 1992
Q15. Municipality?
Ans: 74th Amendment 1992
Q16. Election Commission?
Ans: Article 324
Q17. National Emergency?
Ans: Article 352
Q18. President Rule?
Ans: Article 356
Q19. Financial Emergency?
Ans: Article 360 - Aaj tak nahi laga
Q20. Art 32 ko Heart & Soul kisne kaha?
Ans: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
Q21. Article 44 kya hai?
Ans: Uniform Civil Code (UCC) - DPSP
Q22. Article 370 kis se related tha?
Ans: Jammu & Kashmir ko special status - 5 Aug 2019 ko hataya
Q23. 42nd Amendment ko kya kehte hain?
Ans: Mini Constitution - 1976
Q24. 44th Amendment kya hai?
Ans: 1978 - Right to Property ko Fundamental Right se hataya
Q25. President ka tenure?
Ans: 5 saal - Art 56
Q26. Vice President ko kaun hatata hai?
Ans: Rajya Sabha + Lok Sabha - Art 67
Q27. Lok Sabha ka max strength?
Ans: 552 (550+2 Anglo Indian - ab hat gaya) - ab 550
Q28. Rajya Sabha ka max strength?
Ans: 250 (238 elected + 12 nominated)
Q29. Anti-Defection Law?
Ans: 52nd Amendment 1985, 10th Schedule
Q30. NOTA kab aaya?
Ans: 2013 Supreme Court order se
Q31. Fundamental Rights suspend kab hote hain?
Ans: National Emergency (Art 352) me - Art 20,21 ko chhod ke
Q32. Writs kitne type ke hain?
Ans: 5 type - Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo Warranto
Q33. Attorney General - Article?
Ans: Art 76 - Govt ka lawyer
Q34. Comptroller and Auditor General tenure?
Ans: 6 saal ya 65 saal age tak
Q35. Planning Commission kab hata?
Ans: 2014 - jagah NITI Aayog 1 Jan 2015
Q36. Inter-State Council?
Ans: Article 263
Q37. Zonal Council kitne hain?
Ans: 5 Zonal + 1 North Eastern
Q38. President kis se oath leta hai?
Ans: Chief Justice of India se
Q39. Lok Sabha ka first Speaker?
Ans: G.V. Mavalankar
Q40. Constitution ka Father?
Ans: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar - Chairman Drafting Committee
Q41. Kesavananda Bharati Case?
Ans: 1973 - Basic Structure Doctrine diya
Q42. Maneka Gandhi Case?
Ans: 1978 - Article 21 ka scope badhaya
Q43. Article 32 kiske paas jate hain?
Ans: Supreme Court - Right to Constitutional Remedies
Q44. Article 226?
Ans: High Court ko Writ ka power
Q45. President ki Veto Power?
Ans: 3 type - Absolute, Suspensive, Pocket Veto
Q46. Money Bill kahan se start hota hai?
Ans: Sirf Lok Sabha me - Art 109
Q47. Joint Sitting - Article?
Ans: Art 108 - Speaker preside karta hai
Q48. CAG ko kaun hatata hai?
Ans: President - Supreme Court judge ki tarah
Q49. UPSC ke members ka tenure?
Ans: 6 saal ya 65 saal tak - Art 316
Q50. NITI Aayog ka Chairman?
Ans: Prime Minister
