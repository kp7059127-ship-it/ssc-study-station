About SSC Study Station

SSC Study Station is an educational platform made for SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD students. Our aim is to provide free, high-quality PDFs, Mock Tests, and Previous Year Papers.

We are not affiliated with SSC (Staff Selection Commission). All PDFs are for educational purposes only. We are a student-led initiative based in Lucknow, India.
