📝 English - 50 Grammar Traps

SSC CGL/CHSL 2022-2024 Most Repeated

Q1. He / is junior than / me.
junior to - junior, senior, superior, inferior + to
Q2. Each of the boys / are / present.
is - Each + singular verb
Q3. The sceneries / are beautiful.
scenery is - Uncountable
Q4. I have / many works / to do.
much work
Q5. She is / more better / than you.
better - Double comparative wrong
Q6. He is / the most tallest / boy.
the tallest
Q7. No sooner / did he come / than it started raining.
Correct
Q8. Hardly / had he left / when...
Correct pattern
Q9. Neither Ram / nor Shyam / were there.
was - Verb near nor
Q10. Either of the / two books / are useful.
is
Q11. One of my / friends have / come.
has - One of + plural noun + singular verb
Q12. The number of students / are increasing.
is - The number = singular
Q13. A number of boys / is absent.
are - A number of = plural
Q14. The jury / were divided.
were - Collective as individuals
Q15. I / prefer tea / than coffee.
to - Prefer + to
Q16. He / availed of / the leave.
availed himself of
Q17. She / absented from / class.
absented herself from
Q18. It is / I who is / responsible.
am - Verb as per I
Q19. Lest you / will miss / the train.
should - Lest + should
Q20. He asked / that why / I was late.
Remove that
Q21. He said / that he is / ill.
was - Past reported speech
Q22. If he will come / I will go.
comes - If clause simple present
Q23. I have known him / since three years.
for three years - Since point, for period
Q24. He has been living / from 2020.
since 2020
Q25. He / is suffering / with fever.
from
Q26. He / resembles with / his father.
resembles (no with)
Q27. He / entered into / the room.
entered (no into)
Q28. He / returned back / yesterday.
returned (back extra)
Q29. Despite / of being poor / he helps.
Despite being (no of)
Q30. Though he is rich / but he is / unhappy.
Remove but (Though...yet)
Q31. He / is too weak / to cannot walk.
to walk
Q32. The two first / chapters are easy.
first two
Q33. My all friends / are there.
All my friends
Q34. Let you and / I / do it.
me - Objective after Let
Q35. Between you / and I / it is okay.
me
Q36. Who / did you give / the book?
Whom
Q37. He / as well as / you are wrong.
is - Verb as per first subject
Q38. Not only he / but also his friends / was there.
were (verb near also)
Q39. He / is working / hardly to pass.
hard - Hardly = almost not, Hard = tough
Q40. He / died with / cancer.
of
Q41. He / died by / accident.
in an accident
Q42. I / met him / in the way.
on the way
Q43. He / lives at / Varanasi.
in Varanasi (big city = in)
Q44. He / lives in / Lanka Chowk.
at Lanka Chowk (small place = at)
Q45. He / is married with / her.
to
Q46. He / is angry upon / me.
with me (person) / at mistake
Q47. He / discussed about / the matter.
discussed (no about)
Q48. He / ordered for / tea.
ordered (no for)
Q49. The cost / of this pen / are high.
is
Q50. Many a boy / have / come.
has - Many a + singular verb
